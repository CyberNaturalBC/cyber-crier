🌎 World & Politics
UPDATE
UPDATE: CARNEY LOCKS IN LNG CANADA PHASE 2 — $33B AND UP TO 4,000 KITIMAT JOBS

Prime Minister Mark Carney, speaking in Vancouver Tuesday with B.C. NDP Leader David Eby, welcomed LNG Canada's final investment decision on Phase 2 at Kitimat — a roughly $33-billion private investment he called the second-largest single private investment in Canadian history. CBC and the PMO say it would roughly double export capacity toward 28 million tonnes a year, with up to about 4,000 construction jobs at the plant and Coastal GasLink Phase 2 compressor work adding more.

Both B.C. NDP and Conservatives have pledged more LNG before the Oct. 24 vote; the Greens want a moratorium. Separately, Carney announced $1.2 billion for ocean protection the same day — CityNews notes the timing ahead of an expected West Coast pipeline decision.

Sources (3): CBC · PMO release · CityNews (oceans)
Why it matters: Years of pipe, compressor and skilled-trades work up north — and more fuel for the provincial LNG argument before you vote.
 
TRUTH AND RECONCILIATION DAY — AFTER A MASKED GATHERING NEAR KAMLOOPS

Today is the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation (fifth year). CityNews: Canadians mark survivors and children who never came home; B.C. treats it as a statutory holiday.

CBC and others: Tk'emlúps te Secwépemc reported roughly 30 masked men linked to Second Sons Canada on reserve land Sunday near the former Kamloops residential school. Chief Rosanne Casimir said the community would not be intimidated. Carney called it an "abhorrent" display of hate; Eby and Conservative interim leader Lorne Doerkson both condemned it.

Sources (2): CityNews · CBC
Why it matters: Holiday schedule at home and work; the Kamloops incident is the ugly backdrop local leaders are answering today.

⚔️ Wars & Conflicts
🚨 BREAKING
RUSSIA POUNDS KYIV'S ENERGY GRID; NATO JETS SCRAMBLE IN POLAND AND ROMANIA

ABC News and Ukrainian officials: overnight into Wednesday Russia launched a large drone-and-missile wave — Ukraine's air force cited 188 drones (86 jet-powered) plus ballistic and cruise missiles; 155 drones and five missiles were said intercepted, with impacts at 18 locations and Kyiv the focus. Mayor Klitschko reported multiple dead and injured as tallies moved through the morning; Zelenskyy and DTEK described hits on energy infrastructure and outages, with repair crews restoring power to thousands of homes.

Poland and Romania said they scrambled military aircraft as Russian jets/drones operated near their borders. The Guardian's briefing also notes NATO condemning Moscow's recent nuclear rhetoric after Russia warned it would "deploy its entire arsenal" if Kaliningrad were threatened.

Sources (3): ABC News · Kyiv Independent · The Guardian (briefing)
Why it matters: Winter energy-strike season is back. War-risk shipping and steel stay in the price of materials.

UPDATE: U.S.–IRAN TALKS STILL STUCK OVER HORMUZ AND NUKES

Al Jazeera and BBC-adjacent reporting: indirect U.S.–Iran talks remain stalled over sequencing — Tehran wants Strait of Hormuz reopen steps and sanctions relief first; Washington wants a fuller nuclear package. Trump has already rejected Iran's seven-day Hormuz plan.

SitDeck's Wednesday brief (newsletter) adds that talks have collapsed in practice, that Trump amplified "Iran has lost control of the Strait" messaging, and that two U.S. carrier strike groups (CVN-73, CVN-77) remain in the Arabian Sea — treat the post-midterm large-scale airstrike speculation and exact carrier IDs as unconfirmed unless a named outlet backs them.

Sources (3): Al Jazeera · BBC (Hormuz rejection) · SitDeck brief (newsletter)
Why it matters: Hormuz still moves a huge share of world oil. Diesel for vans and generators stays jumpy until the strait calms.

TESLA OPENS $30 BILLION IN CREDIT LINES AS CAPEX STAYS HOT

SEC CLEARS TESLA RETAIL 'AUTO-VOTE WITH THE BOARD' PROGRAM AHEAD OF ANY SPACEX DEAL TALK

Electrek: Tesla won SEC no-action relief Tuesday for an "Issuer Voluntary Retail Voting Program" — opt-in retail holders can have their shares voted the board's way at every meeting until they cancel. Timing matters because Tesla still has not scheduled its 2026 annual meeting and Musk continues to tease SpaceX-overlap / merger talk that could interact with his pay package's change-in-control math. Participants can override or opt out; Electrek notes the menu only follows the board, not a vote-against option.

Sources (1): Electrek
Why it matters: TSLA holders should read any broker 'opt in' prompt twice before tapping — especially if a SpaceX deal ever hits a ballot.
