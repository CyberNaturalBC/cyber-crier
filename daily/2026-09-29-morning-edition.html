🌎 World & Politics
🚨 BREAKING
LNG CANADA PHASE 2 GETS THE GO-AHEAD — CARNEY IN VANCOUVER TODAY

CBC reports LNG Canada and Shell confirmed the final investment decision for the multibillion-dollar Phase 2 expansion at Kitimat, which would roughly double production from 14 to 28 million tonnes a year and expand Coastal GasLink with five new compressor stations. Prime Minister Mark Carney is in Vancouver Tuesday to frame it as a "historic investment," with a public event at 11 AM PT and an afternoon ocean-protection briefing.

Both B.C. NDP and Conservatives have pledged more LNG on the Oct. 24 trail; the B.C. Greens want a moratorium. Critics flag higher fracking emissions and earthquake risk in the Peace.

Sources (2): CBC · Globe and Mail (resource duel)
Why it matters: Kitimat Phase 2 means years of pipe, compressor and skilled-trades work — and another round in the provincial LNG argument before you vote.
 
U.S. IMPORT BANS ON CANADIAN BOOZE AND MORE NOW LIVE; TRUMP TALKS A DEAL 'WITHIN WEEKS'

CBC says U.S. bans on most packaged Canadian beer, wine and spirits — plus certain whey, molasses and big motorcycles — took effect 12:01 a.m. ET Tuesday (9:01 p.m. PT Monday), hitting B.C. producers on one of the trade war's thorniest fronts. CityNews reports Trump told Oval Office reporters Monday he still expects a Canada trade deal "within weeks" even as the bans landed.

Sources (2): CBC · CityNews / CP
Why it matters: B.C. distillers and grocery shelves feel this first. Watch the loonie and any retaliatory talk that spills into fuel and building materials.

UPDATE: IRAN STRIKE THAT HURT EIGHT MARINES WAS ON A NON-MILITARY SHIP

The Washington Post and Military.com, following NBC, confirm eight U.S. Marines were wounded Sept. 14 when an Iranian cruise missile hit a nonmilitary maritime vessel in the Strait of Hormuz. Injuries included smoke inhalation and possible concussions/TBIs; all returned to duty. The Pentagon had not publicly disclosed the attack at the time.

Trump has rejected Iran's seven-day Hormuz reopen plan. SitDeck's Tuesday brief adds that U.S. Navy/Marine wounded in the Middle East total 38 in September and flags a UK investigation into a possible foreign-linked plot near an airbase used by the U.S. (RAF Fairford assessed) — Iran denied involvement; treat the SitDeck casualty tally and Fairford attribution as unconfirmed unless a named outlet backs the exact numbers.

Sources (4): Washington Post · Military.com · BBC (Hormuz deal) · SitDeck brief (newsletter)
Why it matters: Hormuz still moves a huge share of world oil. Diesel for vans and generators stays jumpy until the strait calms.
 
RUSSIA HITS KYIV'S ACADEMY OF SCIENCES; FRESH BALLISTIC STRIKES OVERNIGHT

Al Jazeera and The Guardian report a Monday daytime Russian drone strike hit Ukraine's National Academy of Sciences in central Kyiv, killing at least two and injuring others; Zelenskyy said nationwide strikes killed at least nine and two dozen-plus were wounded in Kyiv alone. Kyiv Independent and Kyiv Post say ballistic missiles and jet-powered drones hit again overnight into Tuesday, damaging an educational facility and other buildings while air defenses worked overtime.

Sources (3): Al Jazeera · The Guardian · Kyiv Independent
Why it matters: Winter energy-and-city strike season is back. War-risk shipping and steel stay in the price of materials.

🚨 BREAKINGUPDATE
UPDATE: STARSHIP FLIGHT 14 REACHES ORBIT — FIRST OPERATIONAL STARLINK V3 DEPLOY

Space.com, Ars Technica and Spaceflight Now: Starship Flight 14 lifted off from Starbase about 5:48 a.m. PT Monday, reached orbit for the first time despite an early Ship engine-out that briefly looked like a scrub, deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites, then came home early. Controllers cut the planned ~10-hour / six-orbit flight; Ship splashed down intact north of Hawaii about three hours after liftoff, then tipped and exploded on the waves — normal for an unrecovered ocean splashdown. Booster 21 did a controlled Gulf splashdown.

SitDeck's overnight brief wrongly framed the fiery end as a "mission failure"; major space outlets call the orbital insertion and V3 deploy a success.

Sources (3): Space.com · Ars Technica · Spaceflight Now
Why it matters: First orbit plus real V3 sats is the catalyst for denser Starlink — good news for Starlink Mini users on the road. SPCX still sold off (see Markets).
 
TESLA PUSHES ROADSTER DEMO TO OCT. 15; STREET SPLIT ON Q3 DELIVERIES

Sources (2): Electrek (Roadster) · Electrek (Q3 estimates)
Why it matters: TSLA had a rough Monday close. Delivery prints still move the stock for investors — Canada FSD status unchanged in today's sources.

EBY AND DOERKSON DUEL OVER MINERALS AND LNG; POLLS STILL DISAGREE
