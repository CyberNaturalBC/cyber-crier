🚨 BREAKING
🚨 IRAN STEPS UP HORMUZ TANKER ATTACKS JUST AS OIL FLOWS RECOVER

UK Maritime Trade Operations has logged nine attacks in the Strait of Hormuz so far in October, already half of September’s total for Hormuz and the Gulf combined, Bloomberg reports. The worst hit the tanker On Peace off Oman on Tuesday: a fire, 12 seafarers hurt (11 of them Indian) and an Omani air force medevac, World Ports Organization and the Arabian Post report. Gulf exports had climbed back to roughly 80% of pre-war levels under a U.S.-protected route, CNBC notes, and tanker rates to China are now over US$1 million a day. U.S. Vice President JD Vance told Reuters Iran is hitting ships but “not enough to stop the flow,” and said Washington is talking with Iran’s president and foreign minister.

Sources (5): Bloomberg (via Moneyweb) · The Japan Times / Bloomberg · World Ports Organization · CNBC Africa · SitDeck brief (newsletter)
Why it matters: Brent is back near US$102 and diesel stays pricey, which feeds straight into fuel, freight and job costs here.
 
🚨 BREAKING
🚨 ~70 MISSILES ON PUTIN’S BIRTHDAY: 13 DEAD IN A PRYLUKY APARTMENT BLOCK

Russia fired about 70 missiles, many of them ballistic, plus Shahed drones at 12 Ukrainian regions overnight, Zelenskyy says. A five-storey apartment building in Pryluky (Chernihiv region) was destroyed; regional prosecutors put the toll there at 13 dead, including four children, and 50 hurt, and RFE/RL counts 19 dead nationwide. Earlier tallies from Kyiv Post and RBC-Ukraine were lower, so expect the numbers to keep changing. Zelenskyy called it a birthday “gift” to Putin, who turns 74 today; EU chief Ursula von der Leyen accused Moscow of a “strategy of terror.” Energy infrastructure was hit again.

Sources (4): RFE/RL · Interfax-Ukraine · Kyiv Post · The Guardian
Why it matters: Hits on homes and the power grid this early in the fall point to a hard winter for Ukrainians.
 
DRONES SINK A CARGO SHIP IN NATO MEMBER BULGARIA’S BLACK SEA WATERS

Sea and air drones struck two merchant ships about 70 nautical miles off Bulgaria’s coast on Tuesday, inside its exclusive economic zone, Bulgaria’s prime minister said (BTA). The Togo-flagged Alfa Watan sank with its crew still unaccounted for; all 18 crew of the Palau-flagged Able were rescued, Euronews reports. Zelenskyy blamed Russia, but Bulgaria hasn’t confirmed who launched the drones, and its president has called a security council meeting. Separately, a Ukrainian naval drone set a tanker ablaze off Sochi, Russian officials told The Guardian; its 23 Indian crew were rescued.

Sources (4): BTA · Euronews · The Guardian (briefing) · The Guardian (Sochi tanker)
Why it matters: If Russia is confirmed as the attacker, it raises the stakes for NATO, and Canada with it.

🔭 Science & Research
CHEMISTRY NOBEL: HOW LIFE GOT ITS ‘HANDEDNESS’ — KAGAN AND SOAI WIN

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry goes to Henri B. Kagan (Université Paris-Sud, France) and Kenso Soai (Tokyo University of Science) “for the discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis,” the Royal Swedish Academy announced this morning. Many molecules, like amino acids, come in left- and right-handed mirror-image forms, but life uses only one. Science magazine says the Soai reaction was the first to make only one mirror image spontaneously, with no outside chiral help, a clue to how life’s one-handed chemistry began. The BBC notes the drug industry relies on this, since one form of a molecule can heal while its twin does nothing.

Sources (4): NobelPrize.org · BBC · Science · C&EN
Why it matters: Origin-of-life chemistry and cheaper, safer medicines, all in one prize.

🚀 Tesla / SpaceX / Starlink / xAI
MODEL 3 AND MODEL Y CAN NOW BACK UP YOUR HOUSE — WITH CATCHES

Tesla switched on Powershare Home Backup for the Model 3 and Model Y, so they can feed a house during an outage, Electrek and The Verge report. Until now only the Cybertruck could. You need a Powerwall 3 plus a Wall Connector or Universal Wall Connector, and Tesla says a car charged to 90% adds “over two days” of backup for a home using 30 kWh a day. The guarantee only covers cars ordered in the U.S. and Puerto Rico from Oct. 1, 2026. Older cars are “some, but not all,” depending on an inverter part, and owners can check under Software, then Additional Vehicle Information, for “Powershare Support.” Electrek points out that Ford and GM have offered home backup for years.

Sources (4): Electrek · The Verge · Teslarati · InsideEVs
Why it matters: Older Model 3 and Model Y owners should check their screen. Tesla hasn’t mentioned Canada, and it only works with a Powerwall 3.

SURREY: ADVANCE POLLS OPEN TODAY — AND ANNIS PITCHES $555M FOR THE CLOVERDALE FAIRGROUNDS

City of Surrey advance voting runs today, Oct 10, 14 and 15, 8 AM–8 PM, at ten sites including Cloverdale Recreation Centre (6188 176 Street). Bring your voter card and one piece of ID, or two pieces of ID if you’re not registered. Today is also the last day the city mails out vote-by-mail packages. On Tuesday, Linda Annis’s Surrey First slate released a platform with $1.791 billion in capital projects, the Now-Leader reports. That includes $555 million at the Cloverdale Fairgrounds: a $350M multi-sport and aquatic centre, a $150M trade and exhibition centre, a $37M curling rink and an $18M truck museum. Annis also promises 300 more police officers over four years and no property-tax increase in 2027. Rival Doug McCallum (Safe Surrey Coalition) promises a four-year property-tax freeze, and Mayor Brenda Locke (Surrey Connect) is seeking re-election.

Sources (4): City of Surrey — advance voting · City of Surrey — notice · Surrey Now-Leader · Surrey Now-Leader (candidates)
Why it matters: Big build promises for Cloverdale against tax-freeze pledges. Weigh the shiny projects against who pays.
 
LEAKED LETTER: CONSERVATIVES WOULD BRING BACK ACCIDENT LAWSUITS; NDP SAYS RATES WILL SOAR

A Sept. 29 letter from Conservative Leader Lorne Doerkson to groups including the Trial Lawyers Association and the Insurance Bureau of Canada promises a hybrid system that would again let seriously injured crash victims sue, CBC reports. The union group MoveUp leaked it. The party says no-fault would stay for most claims and private insurers could compete with ICBC. CBC spoke with a Cranbrook grandmother who lost three relatives to a logging truck and says no-fault left the family unable to tap its liability coverage. NDP candidates say lawsuits would drive premiums up, and the Western Standard (right-leaning) reports Eby called it a “secret deal” with trial lawyers.

Sources (2): CBC BC · Western Standard (right-leaning)
Why it matters: ICBC premiums hit every B.C. driver and work truck. This is a real money question.
 
CRIME VS. HEALTH: DOERKSON’S 970 JUSTICE HIRES, EBY’S MRIS — NEITHER COSTED

Doerkson promised 500 RCMP officers, 250 more municipal police through a bigger police academy, 50 Crown prosecutors and 20 trial judges with Ottawa’s help, plus “compassionate intervention” to take people with severe addiction into care, an end to safe supply, and bans on drug use in shelters and hospitals (Daily Hive, CHEK).
