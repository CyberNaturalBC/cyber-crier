🌎 World & Politics
OTTAWA PUTS $430 MILLION INTO UNION TRADES TRAINING

The federal government will spend more than $430 million over five years on its Union Training and Innovation Program, Employment and Social Development Canada announced Oct. 15, CBC reports. The money breaks down to $197.2 million for training-centre renovations, expansions and new builds, $135 million for new ways to help apprentices and journeypersons, and $100 million for training equipment, where Ottawa's share temporarily rises from 50% to 70% of the cost, the department says.

Unions and union training trusts can file expressions of interest for building money until Nov. 12; innovation proposals close Dec. 17, and equipment applications are open any time. Canada's Building Trades Unions says the money will help its 14 affiliates and their 500,000 members, and the government says Canada faces more than 1.4 million skilled-trades openings from 2024 to 2033.

Sources (3): CBC News · Canada.ca: program and deadlines · tdotnews (ESDC release)
Why it matters: Union training centres can apply for equipment at a 70% federal share now, and the building money has a Nov. 12 deadline.

IRAN SAYS HORMUZ IS CLOSED AFTER TWO SUPERTANKERS HIT MINES

Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy said Saturday that the Strait of Hormuz is closed to ships it says are breaking its rules, after two supertankers, each carrying about two million barrels of crude, struck naval mines on routes it calls unauthorized, Euronews and Turkey's state news agency Anadolu report. The Guards say both ships caught fire and the second was left dead in the water; those claims come from Iran and haven't been independently confirmed.

The U.S. tells a different story. Central Command's Adm. Brad Cooper said about 400 merchant ships went through the strait in the past week and U.S. forces helped move more than 100 million barrels of crude, according to X news summaries of his remarks. BBC Verify counted 12 ships reporting attacks in a single week earlier this month, a record. Oil was already climbing before this: U.S. crude jumped 3.4% Friday to about US$101.35 a barrel and Brent to about US$109.55, Yahoo Finance reports.

Sources (6): Euronews · Anadolu Agency (Turkish state) · Middle East Eye · BBC Verify · X news summary: CENTCOM (Grok on X) · Yahoo Finance: oil
Why it matters: If tankers stop moving, diesel and gas prices at the pump go up fast, and so do freight costs on everything from building supplies to groceries.

🔭 Science & Research
GOOGLE'S WILLOW CHIP CRACKS A QUANTUM BOTTLENECK, AND BITCOIN TAKES NOTE

Google's Quantum AI team says it has shown 'magic state cultivation' working on real hardware for the first time, cutting errors about 40-fold on its Willow processor, in a paper published in Science on Oct. 15. Magic states are special quantum states a computer needs for the hardest operations, and making clean ones has been a major drain; Google says the method reached at least 99.99% fidelity, though only about 8% of attempts pass its checks. CEO Sundar Pichai called it 'magical progress' on X.

CoinDesk says the result shrinks one of the hardware hurdles that stands between today's machines and cracking old bitcoin wallets, and nine crypto firms have formed a 'Quantum Alliance' to prepare, though they disagree on when the threat arrives. This is a step, not a code-breaker: the paper is about one building block of an error-corrected machine that doesn't exist yet.

Sources (5): Google blog · Science (paper) · @sundarpichai on X · CoinDesk: Willow · CoinDesk: Quantum Alliance
Why it matters: Real progress, but nobody's bitcoin is getting cracked this year; watch how fast the next steps come.
 
ASTRONOMERS FIND THE FIRST GREEN STAR

A cool white dwarf about 280 light-years away in the constellation Draco would look chartreuse green to the human eye, a University of Warwick team led by Mark Hollands reports. Its helium atmosphere is laced with sodium from rocky debris it swallowed, which soaks up yellow, orange and some red light, while calcium trims the violet, leaving green, Warwick says. Normal stars can't be green because their colour is set by temperature; white dwarfs show colours from their chemistry. The team modelled 151 nearby white dwarfs and also found a salmon-pink one coloured by carbon, according to X summaries of the study.

Sources (4): University of Warwick · Phys.org · RAS Techniques and Instruments (paper) · X news summary (Grok on X)
Why it matters: Dead stars that ate their planets let astronomers read what those rocky worlds were made of.
 
FEWER HURRICANES IN A WARMER WORLD, BUT NASTIER ONES

A new Princeton analysis of 14 climate models finds that the more a model warms the planet, the fewer tropical cyclones it produces worldwide, with numbers rising in the northern hemisphere but falling in the southern, physicist Sabine Hossenfelder said in a YouTube video this week. A separate study in npj Climate and Atmospheric Science also projects a 2 to 10% global drop by late century. Hossenfelder notes the UN climate panel's view, held with medium confidence, that storms get fewer or stay the same but grow stronger and wetter: 'more warming means fewer hurricanes, but worse ones.' She says she has limited faith in climate models' exact predictions, but calls this one fairly robust because storm formation is simpler than things like regional drought.

Sources (2): Sabine Hossenfelder (YouTube) · npj Climate and Atmospheric Science (CMIP6 study)
Why it matters: Fewer storms doesn't mean safer; the big ones are what break roofs and supply chains.

BARCLAYS: A TESLA–SPACEX MERGER COULD LAND BEFORE DECEMBER

Barclays analyst Dan Levy says a Tesla–SpaceX merger could be announced before Tesla's annual meeting, which must be held by Dec. 6, Investing.com and Yahoo Finance report. His most likely structure has SpaceX buying Tesla for stock, about 0.7 SpaceX shares per Tesla share based on values of roughly US$2.1 trillion and US$1.5 trillion, plus a premium; Musk would end up with about 37% of the combined company and 56% of the votes, GuruFocus reports. Levy says the real prize is tying together Terafab chips, Robotaxi, Optimus, Starlink and Grok, and flags antitrust, both shareholder votes and national-security questions tied to Tesla's China business.

Neither company has announced anything; X summaries say prediction markets put the odds of a deal within a year around 60%. SpaceX reports its third-quarter results Monday, Oct. 20.

Sources (5): Investing.com · Yahoo Finance · GuruFocus · X news summary (Grok on X) · @SpaceX on X: Q3 date
Why it matters: Tesla shareholders could wake up holding SpaceX stock, and Musk would tighten his grip on both.
